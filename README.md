# Collaborative Study Material & Quiz Platform

A web-based platform for students and instructors to share study materials,
create and take quizzes, discuss topics, and track learning progress.

## Objectives

The platform will support:

- Uploading and organizing notes, slides, PDFs, and links
- Creating and managing quizzes
- Taking quizzes with automatic grading
- Discussions and comments on study materials
- Rating study materials
- Tracking scores and progress
- Leaderboards and analytics
- Notifications
- Admin controls for users and content
